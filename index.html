Forge - Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

1. General information

Forge is an application that provides users with a daily challenge. Every user receives the same challenge.

2. Data collection

Forge does not collect or store personal data. The application does not require creating an account or providing personal information.

3. Local data storage

Forge may store information locally on the user's device, such as challenge progress and application settings. This data remains on the user's device.

4. External services

Forge uses Firebase services provided by Google to deliver current challenges. Firebase processes information according to Google's privacy policy.

5. Data sharing

Forge does not sell, share or transfer user data to third parties.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the email address provided in Google Play.
